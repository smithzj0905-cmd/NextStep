# Phase 1 content starters

Use these as genuinely useful articles/short videos. Do not spam communities.

## 1. I received a confusing government letter. What should I do?

Explain:
- identify the issuing agency
- find the action paragraph
- locate the deadline
- verify the deadline with the agency
- gather requested documents
- keep a copy of the response

CTA: "Still unsure? Upload the letter to NextStep for an informational action checklist."

## 2. What does an insurance cancellation notice mean?

Explain common reasons, effective date, replacement coverage, appeal/contact options, and the importance of verifying the policy directly.

## 3. What does a property tax reassessment notice mean?

Explain assessed value vs. tax bill, appeal windows, and where to verify local requirements.

## 4. What should I look for in a DMV renewal notice?

Explain expiration date, required insurance/documentation, fees, and official renewal channel.

## 5. What is the difference between a notice, bill, request, and deadline?

Teach readers how to classify paperwork before deciding what to do.

Every article should include a prominent "Upload your document" CTA.
